IFX Brand · Logo exploration

Six directions for the IFX mark

Concepts 01 to 03 pair a symbol with the lowercase Outfit wordmark. Concepts 04 to 06 put the idea into the letters of ifx itself. Each is shown in full colour, reversed on black, and at app-icon and favicon sizes.

Symbol and wordmark

01 to 03
CONCEPT 01

Exchange Loop

Two counter-rotating arrows form a continuous circuit: buy and sell, two currencies, one balanced book. The centre dot is the bureau, the point where every trade settles.

On the website now
CONCEPT 02

Spread X

A rising rate line crosses a falling one to form the X in IFX. The gap where they meet is the spread, where a bureau earns its margin. The solid tile works well as an app icon.

CONCEPT 03

Meridian Shield

A globe inside a two-tone shield combines international reach with protection, compliance and AML. The most institutional of the set; suits regulators, banks and enterprise buyers.

Wordmark art

04 to 06
CONCEPT 04

Upstroke

Drawn as one monoline: the crossbar of the f runs straight into the x, and the x's second stroke rises as a rate arrow. The name itself reads like a chart. The dot of the i matches the arrow.

CONCEPT 05

Split-Flap

A single hairline cut runs through the wordmark, like the split in a mechanical rateboard flap on a bureau counter. Upper half in jungle green, lower half in ink. The icon is the flap itself.

CONCEPT 06

Overlap

The x is built from two crossing bars, one for each currency in a pair. Where they overlap, a third colour appears: the exchange. The dot of the i picks up the same green.
